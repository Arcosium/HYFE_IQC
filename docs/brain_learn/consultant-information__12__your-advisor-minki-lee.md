### [LinkedIn Profile](https://linkedin.com/in/minki-lee-abba22205)

- I'm Minki Lee, and I am delighted to introduce myself and extend my excitement to collaborate with you during the BRAIN Research Advisory Program.
- I graduated from Seoul National University with a Bachelor's degree in Business Administration.
- My career began as a co-founder of a O2O service startup, which sparked my interest in understanding the factors that shape a company's value.
- Following this interest, I participated in IQC2023, and was offered an internship opportunity midway through the competition. Later I transitioned to a full-time researcher role.
- Currently, my focus is on researching Alphas in the EUR region, a region less explored by BRAIN consultants. I'm also interested in developing an alpha creation machine to aid in finding Alphas in these less explored areas.
- As a BRAIN researcher, I find it fascinating how collective intelligence adds value and enhances market efficiency. I hope that as consultants, you can gain much from this advisory program😁
- I'll be sending out invites for our calls soon. If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to reach out to me directly via email.
